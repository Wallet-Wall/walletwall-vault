### 4.2 Ordering inside `cancelRecoveryByQuorum`

`_requireQuorum` runs **before** `_consume`, as in every other quorum path of
the kernel: a proof that does not meet quorum leaves the nonce unconsumed
(test A3 measures `nonces[GUARDIAN]` unchanged after `QuorumNotMet`), and the
generation is bound in the digest, so a proof signed under the previous
generation dies as `QuorumNotMet` before the nonce is examined (test E2).

### 4.3 Clarifications that are not deviations

1. **Guard placement in `initiateRecovery`.** The contract says "effectively-live
   request cannot be overwritten" and "reject before consuming nonce"; it does
   not say where. The guard sits immediately after `_requireRecoveryOpen()`, so
   it precedes the `ZeroAddress` checks as well as the digest and `_consume` —
   the same position the measured Candidate C used, and the position that keeps
   `BadState` the first answer for a live slot regardless of argument shape.
2. **Inline tag rather than a named constant.** The forecast's "the only added
   declaration is a `bytes32 private constant`" was a *forecast of storage
   minimality*; a constant occupies no storage either way. The inline literal
   follows the kernel's existing digest-tag convention and adds no declaration.

## 5. SD-4 and SD-10

**SD-4** (`ecdsa-only-shape-declaration-is-uncounted`) is **not** remediated by
W2 and was not meant to be. What W2 changes is the *availability* of the
architecture-native remedy the amendment §4 names: while the mis-shaped request
is live, the guardian quorum cancels it (mechanism B) and proposes a correctly
shaped one; once it has expired, a fresh proposal proceeds directly. Neither
path touches a clock, adds a delay, or introduces a ratification family — A, E
and G′ are not reintroduced. The ledger entry in `stateful/defects.ts` is #188's
and is unchanged; the stateful campaign still records SD-4 hits (3 in run 2) as
known-defect hits, exactly as before.

**SD-10** (`APPROVED_REQUEST_STRANDED_BY_GUARDIAN_ROTATION`) — `setGuardians`
is byte-for-byte unchanged. **Blast radius, recorded not remediated:** a request
stranded by a generation bump is still `active` and not yet expired, so it is
*effectively live*. Before W2 the new quorum could silently overwrite it; under
W2 `initiateRecovery` answers `BadState` and `bindMigration` answers
`NoRecovery` until it expires — **or** until the new quorum clears it with
`cancelRecoveryByQuorum`, whose digest binds the *current* generation, after
which re-initiation succeeds (test H1 executes exactly this sequence and asserts
the epoch is carried, not refunded). Two explicit, observable acts where there
was one silent overwrite. The remediation of SD-10 itself (deny or preserve
under rotation) is unchanged and undecided here.
