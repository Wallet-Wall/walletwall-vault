> none of them. Severity is stated for the Gen-2 use (mandatory PQ conjunct on every authority predicate, verifier
> revert = denial), where an unsound PQ verifier is an asset-loss vulnerability against a quantum adversary holding

## Findings about this lane's own work (recorded so a reviewer does not re-find them)

- The first blob-mutation position chosen (byte 100 of half 0) sits in an ABI length word the reader never loads;
  the mutation was therefore **accepted**. The test now places mutations by content and records that unread
  framing bytes exist (U-4). The review then found a second unread region (half 1's `tr`) and an accepted
  non-canonical layout; both are now in the census.
- The Keccak-f[1600] zero-state known answer was first typed with a wrong nibble; the chain and `@noble` agreed with
  each other throughout, the constant was the error. Corrected to `F1258F7940E1DDE7` (little-endian `e7dde140798f25f1`).
- Under Osaka rules the simulated node refuses any transaction above 16,777,216 gas; the harness's default of
  30,000,000 had to drop to the cap, and the measurement suite now asserts the refusal.
- Contract addresses and the intrinsic gas of calldata holding addresses depend on which suites ran first in the
  shared simulated chain; the records now compare only address-independent figures.
- An earlier draft of `PUBLISHER_BINDING.md` and of U-3 recommended a proof of possession against degenerate keys
  and gated only `t1 == 0`. The review refuted both (A-1); the test now carries the `t1 = 1023` forgery and the
  documents carry the Fireblocks criterion.
- An earlier draft described the expansion pins as "independent"; they are outputs of the same code (review A-6).
  The counts were also wrong (26 pinned by the vendor's file, 26 by the feasibility run; not 41/11).
- "Inside-call gas" is a `gasleft()` delta around a STATICCALL and includes the opcode's own cost, a few thousand
  gas (~0.2 %) above the callee (review A-8). Labels corrected; figures unchanged.
